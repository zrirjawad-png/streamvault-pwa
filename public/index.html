Why did YouTube download an empty video before?

YouTube splits video and audio into separate adaptive streams (DASH). Without ffmpeg audio/video muxing on the backend, piped streams output 0 bytes. StreamVault now automatically merges them and protects against 0-byte files.

Backend & Extraction Proxy

The app appends the video URL and quality parameters. For local testing, use http://localhost:10000/api/stream?url=.

Download Ready-to-Deploy Backend Render & Docker Ready

Includes the fixed server.js with ffmpeg audio/video muxing, Dockerfile, and package.json for free 1-click deployment on Render.com or Docker.

How to deploy for free in 2 minutes:
1. Unzip and push to a GitHub repository.
2. On Render.com, create a new Web Service and select Docker.
3. Paste your public Render URL into the box above. YouTube & Instagram downloads work automatically!

Install StreamVault App

StreamVault can be installed directly onto your phone, tablet, or desktop home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA).
